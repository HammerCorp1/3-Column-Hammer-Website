Hammer FAQ

Hammer is AI that answers car dealership leads by text in under 5 seconds and books appointments in your existing CRM. 2,500+ independent and franchise stores in the US and Canada use it. 4.6 stars across 266 Google reviews.

What does Hammer do?

Hammer texts every new sales lead in under 5 seconds, day or night, follows up for weeks when buyers go quiet, and books the appointment in the CRM you already use. Your team closes the deal.

Does it replace my BDC?

No. Hammer handles the first reply and the follow-up. Your team closes. If a person takes over a thread, you can pause the AI or let it continue when they leave.

Will it work with our CRM?

Yes. Hammer works with the CRM you already have, including DealerCenter, VinSolutions, DealerSocket, eLead, CDK, Reynolds, DriveCentric, and Tekion. Appointments write into it. No CRM switch.

Which lead sources does Hammer answer?

SMS, Facebook Messenger, Facebook Marketplace, Facebook and TikTok ad leads, OfferUp, Craigslist, webchat, OEM leads, and third-party providers such as CarGurus, AutoTrader, Cars.com, CarFax, Edmunds, and TrueCar.

Does Hammer make AI phone calls?

No. Hammer transcribes calls to the dealership and follows up by text. It does not place AI voice calls.

How fast can we go live?

Most stores go live in under 48 hours once onboarding and feeds are connected. Onboarding is hands-off: Hammer's team connects your CRM and lead vendors.

What do you need from us to start?

Your dealership website, which CRM you run, which lead sources you want live first, and a contact who can approve vendor access.

Can we change what the AI says?

Yes. You can edit any follow-up and its timing in the dashboard, and change playbooks and tone yourself.

How much does Hammer cost?

See the pricing guide at hammertime.com/guides/pricing. There is no per-user fee.

Is Hammer the same as HammerAI or hammer.ai?

No. Hammer (Hammer Corp) builds AI for car dealerships at hammertime.com. HammerAI at hammerai.com is a consumer roleplay chat app. We are not that product, and we are not hammer.ai.
