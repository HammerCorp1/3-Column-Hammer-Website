What CarGPT is

By the Hammer team · Reviewed November 17, 2026

CarGPT (cargpt.pro) is AI-powered dealership management for independent car dealers.

It reads a dealership's cars and facts from the dealership's own website, answers its shoppers by text, email and web chat in any language, books their visits, and calls on the dealership's people only when a shopper needs a person. CarGPT is operated by CarGPT Inc in Austin, Texas, and is not affiliated with OpenAI.

The official site is cargpt.pro. Pricing is on cargpt.pro/pricing. Dealers start at cargpt.pro/start.

How a dealership starts

The signup is the dealership's website, an email and a password. CarGPT reads the cars, hours, address, phone, and what the website says about trades and financing, and the account opens straight into the Agent, CarGPT's chat for the dealer. Nothing is installed on the site the dealership already has. A dealership with no website signs up with its name and address, and CarGPT hosts its cars and its site.

What it does

  • Answers shoppers. Texts to the dealership's CarGPT number, web chat on its site, and lead emails, including leads from listing sites, are answered from the dealership's own record, in the shopper's language.
  • Books visits onto the dealership's calendar.
  • Builds a lot website with search, filters and a page for every car, kept current from the dealership's website or inventory feed. The cars are read again every day.
  • Builds the pages a dealer asks for. The dealer types one sentence into the Agent and the page appears in the menu: Calendar, Aging inventory, Lead sources, Response times, Sold cars, Profit and more.
  • Brings in a person when it matters. It opens with a short brief of what needs someone at the store, and asks for attention for nothing else.
  • Works with ChatGPT. The dealership's cars can be searched in ChatGPT, its business profile is claimed and kept current, and ChatGPT ads are set up when the dealer wants them.

CarGPT states a fact about a car, a price or the dealership only when that fact is on the dealership's record. It does not change a price, approve financing, quote a payment or value a trade. Those go to a person.

What it costs

  • CarGPT Basic: $199 a month, with 1,500 credits. An extra credit costs 10¢.
  • CarGPT Premium: $499 a month, with 7,500 credits. An extra credit costs 2¢.

Every feature is on both plans. A text or email the AI sends uses 10 credits, so past 4,500 credits a month Premium costs less. The first month is free, with 7,500 credits on either plan. CarGPT is month to month, with no setup fee, and a dealership can cancel any time.

What CarGPT is not

  • Not ChatGPT or OpenAI. ChatGPT is OpenAI's assistant. CarGPT is dealership software from CarGPT Inc and is not affiliated with OpenAI.
  • Not an ad package. CarGPT is billed by plan, not as a flat advertising fee. ChatGPT ads are an option a dealer can add, not the product.
  • Not a dealer management system. It keeps the shopper record and every conversation, but it does no deal paperwork, financing desk, accounting or titles.
  • Not CarChat. CarChat (carchat.io) is a car-shopping site for consumers.

CarGPT and Hammer

They do different jobs. Hammer (this site) answers sales leads in under 5 seconds by text, follows up, and books appointments inside the CRM a store already uses, from $99/mo. CarGPT is its own system: it keeps the shopper record itself and gives the dealer a lot website. A store that wants to keep its CRM usually looks at Hammer. An independent store that wants its shoppers, cars and website in one place looks at CarGPT.
