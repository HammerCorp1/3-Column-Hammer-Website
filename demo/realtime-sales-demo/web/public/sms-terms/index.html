Hammer SMS Terms

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Hammer Corp (“Hammer”) sends SMS notifications to its existing dealership clients. These terms explain what we send, how you agree to receive it, and how to stop.

What we send

  • Account notifications, such as onboarding and setup confirmations for your Hammer account
  • Event alerts, such as new leads or messages in your account
  • One-time passcodes (OTPs) for login

Messages are sent from short code 426637 and toll-free number (888) 448-8318 to subscribed phone numbers. We do not send marketing messages from these numbers.

Example message:

Hammer: Your Hammer account setup is in progress. This message confirms texting is working for your dealership. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out. Msg and data rates may apply.

How you opt in

Dealership clients agree to receive SMS notifications by accepting the Hammer Terms of Use when they sign up for Hammer, and by providing a mobile phone number for their account. The SMS Notifications section of the Terms of Use describes this program.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies based on account activity. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You can also remove your number from your Hammer dashboard. After you opt out, we will send one confirmation message and no further texts unless you opt back in.

Help

Reply HELP to any message, or contact customer care at support@hammertime.com or (512) 883-1336.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
